# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

PortLink is a volunteer, pro-bono project built by the Lower Alabama AI community in service
of a maritime ministry. We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming
environment for everyone, regardless of experience level or background. Many contributors here
are learning, and that is celebrated.

## Our standards

We expect everyone in this community to:

- Be welcoming, patient, and kind — especially toward newcomers.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, skill levels, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people it serves.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Behavior that harasses, demeans, excludes, or intimidates others is not welcome, and neither
is any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Standard adopted

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, as its full code of conduct. Please read it for the complete standards and
enforcement guidelines.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project
maintainers through the Lower Alabama AI community or via GitHub. All reports will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of reporters will be respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take appropriate, fair corrective
action — including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal from the project — in
response to behavior they deem inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests,
and discussions) and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
